      THE RULES ARE SIMPLE

      Find your way.
      Beat their time.

      1. 01

        Meet in the maze

        Share a race link. You take A; your friend takes B. Both players ready up.

      2. 02

        Change the maze

        Each side has a switch. Flipping it opens a shortcut gate and closes another — for both players. Your shortcut might be theirs too.

      3. 03

        Reach the other side

        The first valid finish received by the server wins. A new maze appears every day; today’s best times are kept, and every finished race can be replayed.
