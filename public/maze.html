How to play

  1. 01

    Meet in the maze

    Share a race link. You start at portal A, your rival at B. Both press ready for a shared countdown.

  2. 02

    Change the maze

    Stand on a glowing crystal and press E. Its energy gates swap: one opens, another closes — for both players. Amber barred gates open and close on their own every few seconds; they flicker just before they shut.

  3. 03

    Reach their portal

    First valid finish wins. A new maze appears every day; best times are kept and every race can be replayed.
