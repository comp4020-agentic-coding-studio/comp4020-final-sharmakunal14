      How to play

      1. 01

        Meet in the maze

        Share a race link. You start at portal A, your rival at B. Both press ready for a shared countdown.

      2. 02

        Change the maze

        Stand on a glowing crystal and press E. Its energy gates swap: one opens, another closes — for both players.

      3. 03

        Reach their portal

        First valid finish wins. A new maze appears every day; best times are kept and every race can be replayed.

      W A S D / arrows
      Move. In 1st and 3rd person, W is forward and A/D turn.
      E / Space
      Flip the switch you are standing on
      V
      Cycle camera: map · 3rd person · 1st person
